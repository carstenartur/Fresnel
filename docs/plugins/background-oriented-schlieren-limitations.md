# BOS target limitations

The generated target is a deterministic synthetic dot field, not a calibration of a particular display, printer, lens or camera. `intendedDpi` documents intended use and does not prove physical dot size. Printer scaling, optical distortion, display interpolation, motion, exposure and camera demosaicing can alter the captured pattern.

The current slice therefore makes no claim about measurable displacement accuracy. Such claims require reference/disturbed captures, physical calibration, a specified estimator, confidence diagnostics and validation against known displacements.
