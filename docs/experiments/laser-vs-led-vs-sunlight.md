# Laser vs LED vs sunlight

Different sources change contrast, brightness and measured focus stability.

| Source | Spectrum width | Typical observation | Practical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laser pointer | Very narrow | Brightest, highest-contrast focus; speckle visible | Best for first alignment and diagnostics |
| LED (single color) | Moderate | Clean focus with less speckle, slightly wider spot | Good everyday measurement source |
| Sunlight | Broad (white) | Bright but chromatically smeared focus; sensitive to alignment | Good outdoor demo and energy tests |

## Expected differences

- **Laser**: easiest to find first focus, but speckle can make spot texture grainy.
- **LED**: reduced speckle; focus can appear smoother and slightly larger.
- **Sunlight**: strongest total power, but broad spectrum increases chromatic blur.

## Suggested sequence

1. Align with low-power laser or narrow-band LED.
2. Confirm focal distance with LED.
3. Repeat under sunlight to see chromatic behavior in real conditions.

Always follow [Safety notes](safety.md), especially for direct sunlight and
specular reflections.
