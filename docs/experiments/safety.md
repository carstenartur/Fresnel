# Safety notes

These experiments involve concentrated light. Treat every setup as potentially
eye-hazardous.

## Laser safety

- Prefer low-power visible lasers for alignment.
- Never look into a direct or focused beam.
- Never point beams at people, animals, vehicles or aircraft.
- Keep beam path below or above eye level and terminate it on a matte beam stop.

## Sunlight safety

- Concentrated sunlight can damage eyes and heat/ignite materials.
- Never use optical elements to focus sunlight near eyes or unattended surfaces.
- Avoid reflective benches; use matte black or diffuse backgrounds.
- Stop immediately if materials heat up, smoke, or discolor.

## Reflective-surface hazards

- Shiny tools, rulers, clips and foil edges can create unexpected secondary beams.
- Remove unnecessary reflective objects from the optical path.
- Angle mounts to avoid retro-reflections toward the operator.

## Lab practice checklist

- Clear beam path and controlled test area
- Eye-safe alignment habits before power/sun exposure
- Non-flammable mounting materials near high-intensity focus
- Supervised operation only; no unattended focused-beam setups
